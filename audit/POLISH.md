# LS'Eau — Final Polish (Phase 3)

Date: 2026-11-25. Final refinement milestone after Phase 1 (reconstruction) and
Phase 2 (visual identity). Art direction, typography, palette, photography,
motion and content are unchanged. Only the items below were modified.

## 1. AMO — compressed text (root-cause fix)

**Cause.** `.study-grid--steps .study-card` declared a two-column grid
(`4.5rem 1fr`) while each card had three direct children: the index, the `<h3>`
and the paragraph. CSS Grid auto-placement therefore put the paragraph in the
4.5rem index column, producing 2–3-word lines.

**Fix.** The title and paragraphs are now wrapped in a single
`.study-card__body` element (generated by `study_card(..., body_wrapper=True)`
only for the `study-grid--steps` variant). The body occupies the `1fr` column
with `min-width: 0`; paragraphs are capped at `68ch` and headings at `42ch`.
No margin/padding workaround and no content change.

Measured paragraph width / estimated lines: 630 px / 3 lines at 1440, 1024 and
820; 252 px / 4–5 lines at 360. Verified visually at all five widths.

## 2. Eaux pluviales — reduced footprint

The photograph is **not** replaced, sharpened, upscaled or aggressively
cropped. Its container is now a restrained editorial band (max-width 960 px,
centred in the normal container), using the image's native `1413 / 576`
aspect ratio, with a caption below rather than an overlay. At 1440 px the
rendered band is 960 × 391 px (previously ≈ 1440 × 617 full-bleed), a ~37 %
reduction in height with no distortion. Height/width at the tested widths:
391 / 960 (1440), 386 / 948 (1024), 303 / 744 (820), 141 / 346 (390),
129 / 316 (360). Aspect ratio is preserved (verified programmatically).

Documented in `README.md` §7.

New `.notfound` section: oversized outlined `404`, topographic contours,
horizontal geological strata, scientific coordinates, serif headline and an
italic line (“Cette page s'est perdue dans la nappe.”). It keeps a plain
